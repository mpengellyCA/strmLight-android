A patch release: StrmLight streams only from its own StrmQt, takes its picture and sound settings from StrmQt, and looks like StrmQt from the home screen to the stream.

StrmLight 0.9.1 needs **StrmQt 0.9.1 or newer**. Update StrmQt first: an older StrmQt is refused with a message saying so.

## Install

Install `StrmLight-0.9.1.apk` over 0.9.0 (`adb install -r StrmLight-0.9.1.apk`, or through *Downloader* on a Fire TV). It keeps your pairing.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `StrmLight-0.9.1.apk` | The app, for every Fire TV and Android TV (Android 5.0 or newer) |
| `StrmLight-0.9.1.apk.sha256` | Its SHA-256, to check the download |

## What's new

- **Only StrmQt's own Sunshine.** StrmQt names its Sunshine's certificate over its pinned remote, and StrmLight streams from no other: not a plain Sunshine, and not the desktop's own Sunshine on the same computer.
- **One StrmQt per TV.** Pairing with a StrmQt makes StrmLight forget any it was paired with before. Several TVs can still pair with the same StrmQt.
- **Picture and sound come from StrmQt.** Resolution, frame rate, quality, codec and sound (stereo, 5.1, 7.1) are set in StrmQt (Settings › StrmLight › Picture and sound) and read before every stream; StrmLight greys those settings out. *Automatic* codec is HEVC wherever the TV decodes it, H.264 otherwise.
- **Follows StrmQt to its new ports.** StrmQt 0.9.1 moves its Sunshine to 8330–8356; StrmLight finds it there by itself.
- **The connecting screen is StrmQt's**, not Moonlight's grey spinner, and names the computer it is connecting to. Back still cancels.
- **A TV banner** in StrmQt's look for Android TV and Google TV home screens. Fire TV shows a sideloaded app's square icon on a grey tile whatever the app asks for, so there it stays the icon.
- **No host list.** Moonlight's list of computers, its app grid and its shortcuts are gone: StrmLight streams StrmQt only.

## Known limitations

- **Fire TV is slow to show a new icon.** It caches app tiles and refreshes them on its own time, even across a reinstall and a reboot.
- **Not in any store**, so it does not update itself. Install the next release's APK over this one.
- **The host's name is the computer's name**; StrmQt has no setting for a friendlier one yet.
