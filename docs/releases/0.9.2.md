TVs watching one StrmQt together now play in step: two rooms sound as one, with no echo, and each TV keeps its picture with its sound.

Playing together needs **StrmQt 0.9.2** on the computer. With StrmQt 0.9.1, StrmLight 0.9.2 streams as before, each TV as soon as it can.

## Install

Install `StrmLight-0.9.2.apk` over 0.9.1 (`adb install -r StrmLight-0.9.2.apk`, or through *Downloader* on a Fire TV). It keeps your pairing.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `StrmLight-0.9.2.apk` | The app, for every Fire TV and Android TV (Android 5.0 or newer) |
| `StrmLight-0.9.2.apk.sha256` | Its SHA-256, to check the download |

## What's new

- **Plays in step with the other TVs.** StrmLight follows StrmQt's clock (UDP 8339) and tells StrmQt how long its own path is, from the computer to its speaker. Every TV is told the same delay, the slowest TV's path plus a little margin, and plays each sound at that moment. Two TVs side by side are heard as one.
- **The picture follows the sound.** Each picture is held by the same delay and shown when it is due, so lip sync is as it was.
- **Joining is quiet.** A TV that starts streaming while others play shows *Syncing with other devices…*, muted, for a few seconds, and comes in in step; the TVs already playing hear nothing.
- **The menus are as quick as before.** The delay applies only while StrmQt plays something.
- **A stream that fails to start no longer closes the app.**

## Known limitations

- **Every TV waits for the slowest.** Bluetooth speakers or a soundbar on one TV can add a few hundred milliseconds, and then every TV plays that far behind. The performance overlay shows the delay and this TV's own share of it.
- **Starting and stopping a video is heard once on every TV**, as each moves between the menus' no delay and the shared one.
- **Tried on two Fire TVs**, a Fire TV set and a Fire TV Stick, on one network.
- **Not in any store**, so it does not update itself. Install the next release's APK over this one.
