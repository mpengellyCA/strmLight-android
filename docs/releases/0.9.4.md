A patch release: a TV that joins the streaming host while its screen is still gets its picture at once, not a green screen for up to 20 seconds.

The fix needs **StrmQt 0.9.3** on the computer. StrmLight 0.9.4 also streams from, and plays together with, StrmQt 0.9.2, just without the fix.

## Install

Install `StrmLight-0.9.4.apk` over 0.9.3 (`adb install -r StrmLight-0.9.4.apk`, or through *Downloader* on a Fire TV). It keeps your pairing and your Sound offset.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `StrmLight-0.9.4.apk` | The app, for every Fire TV and Android TV (Android 5.0 or newer) |
| `StrmLight-0.9.4.apk.sha256` | Its SHA-256, to check the download |

## What's new

- **A TV that joins gets its picture.** The computer's desktop sends the streaming host a new frame only when something on the screen changes, so a TV that joined while the screen was still could stay green until something moved, for up to 20 seconds, most often soon after the host started. StrmLight now tells the host, four times a second, while it has no picture yet, and StrmQt 0.9.3 draws its screen again each time until it has.

## Known limitations

- **Every TV waits for the slowest.** Bluetooth speakers or a soundbar on one TV can add a few hundred milliseconds, and then every TV plays that far behind.
- **Starting and stopping a video is heard once on every TV**, as each moves between the menus' no delay and the shared one.
- **A change of Sound offset reconnects the stream.**
- **Not in any store**, so it does not update itself. Install the next release's APK over this one.
