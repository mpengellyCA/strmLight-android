A patch release for TVs playing together: a TV that joins no longer holds every TV further behind, and each TV's sound can be fine-tuned by ear.

Playing together needs **StrmQt 0.9.2** on the computer, as for 0.9.2.

## Install

Install `StrmLight-0.9.3.apk` over 0.9.2 (`adb install -r StrmLight-0.9.3.apk`, or through *Downloader* on a Fire TV). It keeps your pairing. Update every TV: one still on 0.9.2 can raise the delay for all of them.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `StrmLight-0.9.3.apk` | The app, for every Fire TV and Android TV (Android 5.0 or newer) |
| `StrmLight-0.9.3.apk.sha256` | Its SHA-256, to check the download |

## What's new

- **A TV that joins leaves the delay alone.** Every TV's picture stalls for about half a second when a TV joins, and a stream's first picture arrives late. StrmLight 0.9.2 reported those moments as its latency, so each join raised the delay for every TV until the video stopped (to 800 ms with four TVs). Now nothing is reported in a stream's first 10 seconds, and the picture reports how late it typically is, not its single latest moment. A joining TV stays on *Syncing with other devices…* until both its sound and its picture are counted.
- **Sound offset.** Settings › Sound offset plays this TV's sound a little earlier or later than it measures, from 50 ms later to 500 ms earlier, by 5 ms near zero. It is for delay Android cannot see: the TV's own sound processing, a soundbar or receiver, or a Dolby re-encode. Pick earlier while this TV is heard after the others, later while it is heard before them. A Fire TV Stick 4K Max needed 5 ms later.

## Known limitations

- **Every TV waits for the slowest.** Bluetooth speakers or a soundbar on one TV can add a few hundred milliseconds, and then every TV plays that far behind.
- **Starting and stopping a video is heard once on every TV**, as each moves between the menus' no delay and the shared one.
- **A change of Sound offset reconnects the stream.**
- **Not in any store**, so it does not update itself. Install the next release's APK over this one.
